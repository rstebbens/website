Story Telling for Influence

Do you ever…

Grapple with getting your point across clearly?
Feel like you’re losing your audience halfway through?
Watch your ideas land… and then quietly disappear?
Find yourself repeating the same thing, hoping it sticks this time?
Struggle to influence decisions or shift people’s thinking?
Get jittery when speaking to a group or wider audience?
Feel like what you’re saying just isn’t connecting?

Storytelling That Actually Lands

(the slightly unexpected bit)

I’ve become a strong advocate for storytelling at work.

Not the fluffy kind, but the kind that actually helps people influence, explain decisions, and make things stick. Because if no one remembers what you said, it didn’t land… no matter how good the content was.

A 4-part workshop series for people who need their ideas to stick

Everyone tells stories.  Most of them drift past unnoticed.

This series is about changing that. Not by turning you into a performer, but by helping you understand what makes people lean in… and what quietly pushes them away.

Across four sessions, we move from raw, unstructured experience to stories that hold attention, create clarity, and influence decisions in real-world environments.

Session 1 – Finding the Story

You’ll start by working with something real. A moment, a situation, a piece of work that mattered.

We focus on identifying stories worth telling and their true message filtering through noise to find the signal and understanding what makes a story relatable, not just “true”

By the end, you’ll have a story on the page and a clearer sense of what it’s actually about beneath the surface.

Session 2 – Making It Work

This is where most stories fall apart. Too much detail. Not enough clarity. No real movement.

We work on structuring your story so it flows and builds naturally strengthening emotional connection without overdoing it and spotting where your story loses the audience

You’ll refine your story and get feedback that helps you see what others actually hear, not what you think you said.

Session 3 – Telling Stories in a Work Context

Telling a story to friends is easy.
 Telling one in a meeting where people are checking Teams and thinking about deadlines… different game.

We explore how to make stories land in business environments balancing clarity, credibility, and engagement turning stories into tools for alignment and decision-making. 

You’ll practise telling your story in a way that fits real-world conversations, not just a workshop setting.

Session 4 – Telling It Without the Safety Net

No notes. No scripts. Just you and the story.  Ever been in a presentation where you've felt like the organiser is just reciting from notes.  Or have you seen death-by-power-point where the host walks your through a painful slide by slide experience reading every slide in detail. 

This session is about delivering with confidence and presence and avoiding predictable patterns and “safe” habits and instead pulling together an engaging presentation that holds peoples attention

You’ll tell your story, get live feedback, and see how far it’s come.
