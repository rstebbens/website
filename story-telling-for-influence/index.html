I’ve become a strong advocate for storytelling at work.

Not the fluffy kind, but the kind that actually helps people influence, explain decisions, and make things stick. Because if no one remembers what you said, it didn’t land… no matter how good the content was.

A 4-part workshop series for people who need their ideas to stick

This series is about changing that. Not by turning you into a performer, but by helping you understand what makes people lean in … and what quietly pushes them away.

Across four sessions, we move from raw, unstructured experience to stories that hold attention, create clarity, and influence decisions in real-world environments.
