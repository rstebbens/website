I'm Rob, an Organisational Coach from the UK.

I've spent over 30 years in IT, which mostly means I've seen the same problems in different outfits, with slightly better slide decks each time.

Most organisations don't struggle because people aren't working hard. They struggle because decisions are unclear, ownership is fuzzy, and everyone's just about aligned, but not quite enough to move.

That's usually where I step in.
