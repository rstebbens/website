Agile Adventures

Step into the shoes of Dave — a battle-hardened yet caffeine-dependent Agile adventurer — as he navigates the absurd perils of modern delivery life. From flaming stand-ups to mutinous retros, you’ll juggle health, charisma, and sanity while making choices that actually shape your fate.

🎯 Over 240 branching options keep each play-through unpredictable.
🏆 7 hidden achievements reward clever play, bad luck, or just surviving the week.
💥 Face bosses like Lord Outputus, the Flow Beast, and the dreaded Phoenix Project.
☕ Recover in safe zones like the Lean Coffee Queue — if you can find them.

Your mission? Survive the sprint, keep your team (and yourself) intact, and maybe… just maybe… become Agile AF.
