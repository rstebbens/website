My Book

Agility, learning and practical experiments.

I wrote a book...

Everyone's writing a book these days

Mostly they are about:  

  • Advice.

  • Frameworks 

  • Ten-step plans to a happier heathier you  

But not the actual you. A different you all togther, because you know that you, is a bit of a dick really.....

Turning 50 left me with several things.  A wife I love very much,  grey hairs and a 9 year old son. 

This book deals with the last one. And a question.   

What would I want my 9 year old to know at 30,  or 40, or hopefully, when he turns 50?  

Taking moments from work where things look obvious in hindsight but felt anything but that, 

Moments from relationships that only made sense years later.

Moments from mountains, bikes, and seemingly everyday decisions that didn’t feel important at the time.   

But now can be seen more clearly. 

This isn't a self help book or about frameworks or gantt charts.  

In fact, it's more about what a 9 year-old boy taught me instead.
